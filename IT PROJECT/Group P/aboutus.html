University Background

Capitol University is a private, non-sectarian co-educational higher education institution
located in Cagayan de Oro City, Philippines. Founded in 1971 by Madame Laureana San Pedro
Rosales as Cagayan Capitol College, the institution originally offered basic secondary and
tertiary programs before rapidly expanding into maritime, engineering, and nursing fields
throughout the 1970s. The Commission on Higher Education (CHED) officially granted the
institution university status in 2003, renaming it Capitol University. Today, it holds a
prestigious Autonomous Status from CHED, with its College of Education recognized as a Center
of Excellence and its information technology, criminology, and civil engineering programs
designated as Centers of Development. The university also operates the Capitol University
Medical Center, which serves as the primary base training hospital for its medical and nursing
students.
